CHOTTUSS Website V3

Music starts after tapping the entrance button.
Without music.mp3, the website plays a short original instrumental ambience generated by the browser (not Christina Perri).
To play A Thousand Years, add an audio file you are authorised to use named music.mp3 in the same folder as index.html. The website tries to begin near 60 seconds into the track.
If your browser blocks audio, tap the Music button again.
